# U.V.S. Nirmana

I still prefer Opus Magnum's scoring system, over Nirmana's.

I feel like factoring in wire cost or some other out of the box metric would
have made this game even better than just doing 'width', or 'height'.

The last metric makes sense, number of logic blocks placed on the bottom board
to redirect signals to other modules is a decent goal to reduce complexity there.

I like how it incentives multiple solutions to the same problem to hit the best 
marker on each of the metrics.



## Warning: Spoilers/Solutions

Somewhat optimized solutions:

### Mathura Nova
