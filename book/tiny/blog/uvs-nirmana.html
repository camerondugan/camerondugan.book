U.V.S. Nirmana

I still prefer Opus Magnum’s scoring system, over Nirmana’s.

I feel like factoring in wire cost or some other out of the box metric would have made this game even better than just doing ‘width’, or ‘height’.

The last metric makes sense, number of logic blocks placed on the bottom board to redirect signals to other modules is a decent goal to reduce complexity there.

I like how it incentives multiple solutions to the same problem to hit the best marker on each of the metrics.

Warning: Spoilers/Solutions

Somewhat optimized solutions:

Mathura Nova

Redirect random same colored input and have the output channels always have one dedicated color.

K.N. Uda

Take two signal sources and dynamically directing them into up to two of three outputs.

SMO

Toggle at two different frequencies only during input signal high/low at different speeds.

Invakan-3

Identify ways to redirect output to different channels based on timing resetting on some unknown signal.
